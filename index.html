How to read the bands

Like with like. A sterling strike, a euro strike, a zloty strike, and a payment per megawatt are different objects. This board does not build one EU average.

  • A · Fixed-bottom energy CfD. UK AR4, AR5, and AR6; France AO4 and AO8; Ireland ORESS 1. UK figures stay in real 2012 pounds.
  • B · Floating energy CfD. France AO5 and AO6; UK AR6 floating (and the small AR4 floating pot). Not mixed into Band A.
  • C · Italy scheme reference. 185 €/MWh ceiling. Not an award, and not a bar on the charts.
  • D · Poland. Published in PLN. Any euro figure is labelled indicative and is not plotted.
  • E · Capacity, permit, or aid-free. Germany €/MW, Netherlands €/year, Denmark Thor. Context only — never on the €/MWh charts.

Research date: 2026-09-21 (Europe/Berlin).

Method: like-with-like by scheme type. Prices stay in the units in which they were published. UK CfD figures stay in real 2012 pounds. There is no single EU average €/MWh. Germany, the Netherlands, and Denmark use other metrics and are kept off the €/MWh charts.

Disclaimer: 185 €/MWh is the FER 2 Allegato 1 base d’asta (reference ceiling), not an Italian strike and not an awarded price. Italy has no published E-1 offshore clearing in 2021–2026. Null and failed rounds are market discipline, not zeroes. Belgium and Spain are coverage gaps, not zeroes.
